How to use Excel to PDF

From workbook to paginated PDF in four steps.

  1. 1

    Upload the workbook

    Add an .xlsx, .xls or .csv file. It is parsed locally with SheetJS, so even private financial data stays on your machine.

  2. 2

    Pick your sheets

    Every sheet appears as a chip. Choose one, or switch to “All sheets” to export the whole workbook into a single PDF.

  3. 3

    Tune the table

    Set orientation (landscape suits wide tables), font size, gridlines and header styling. The preview updates as you go.

  4. 4

    Export the PDF

    Press “Export PDF”. Wide tables shrink to fit or continue on the next page, and header rows repeat automatically.
