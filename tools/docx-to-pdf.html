How to use DOCX to PDF

Three steps from .docx to a paginated PDF.

  1. 1

    Upload your .docx

    Drag the file onto the drop zone or press “Choose files”. Files up to 50 MB are accepted and validated before anything else happens.

  2. 2

    Check the preview

    The document is converted to HTML instantly and rendered at the page size you picked, so you can confirm headings, tables and images look right.

  3. 3

    Download the PDF

    Press “Convert to PDF”. The PDF is generated in your browser and saved to your downloads folder — nothing is uploaded.
