How to use Word Counter

Instant feedback on anything you write.

  1. 1

    Start writing

    Type or paste straight into the box, or drop a .txt file in to analyse existing text.

  2. 2

    Read the statistics

    Every count updates as you type — words, characters with and without spaces, sentences, paragraphs and lines.

  3. 3

    Check the timings

    Reading time assumes 225 words per minute and speaking time 140, so you can plan an essay or a talk.

  4. 4

    Export if you need to

    Copy the text or download it as a .txt file. Nothing is saved on our side.
