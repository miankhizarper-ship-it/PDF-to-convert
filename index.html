Frequently asked questions

Yes. FileTools is a static site with no backend: there is no server endpoint your file could be sent to. The JavaScript libraries do the conversion inside your browser tab, and the output is created as a local download. You can verify it yourself in the Network tab of your developer tools.

Each tool states its own limit (usually 50 MB for documents and images, 100 MB for PDFs). Because everything runs in memory, the real ceiling is your device's RAM — very large PDFs with hundreds of scanned pages may take longer or need a 64-bit browser.

Documents: DOCX to PDF, XLSX/XLS/CSV to PDF, TXT to PDF. Images: JPG, PNG, WEBP (plus GIF and BMP as input for conversion). PDFs: merge, split, and export pages to PNG or JPG. Utilities: QR codes (PNG/SVG) and a live word counter.

The layout is mobile-first and every control is touch-friendly from 320 px up to 4K. Once a page and its libraries are loaded you can keep converting without a connection — but the conversion libraries themselves are fetched from a CDN on first load, so you need internet for that first visit.

Browsers cannot replicate Word's exact pagination engine, so page breaks and some advanced styling (headers/footers, text boxes, tracked changes) can shift. Headings, bold/italic, lists, tables, links and images are preserved, and the live preview shows you the result before you download.
